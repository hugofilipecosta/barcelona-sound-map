## What To Borrow

From the Neobrutalism portfolio:

- dark page background
- orange control surfaces
- hard black borders
- compact centered navigation/control clusters
- simple component shapes with no ornamental polish
- stark black/white/orange contrast
- direct, practical typography

From Product Brain:

- trust before abundance
- source/freshness visibility
- search-first product surface
- mobile-ready scanning
- personalization through explicit filters, not hidden inference

## Core Tokens

- background: black
- primary surface: white
- text on surface: black
- text on background: white
- accent: orange
- border: black on surfaces, white on global chrome
- radius: 0
- shadow: none by default
- borders: thick, visible, structural

## Component Rules

- Panels are white blocks on black.
- Controls use square edges and thick borders.
- Primary actions are orange with black text.
- Result rows must remain dense and scannable.
- Source chips are explicit, visible, and bordered.
- Mobile should preserve the same visual system, not collapse into generic app styling.

## Avoid

- gradients as primary styling
- soft cards
- rounded pill-heavy UI
- decorative blobs
- stock nightlife imagery
- Hatch Conf aesthetics
- Manychat product aesthetics
- design-system showcase aesthetics
